Simplified admission procedure. Students who completed 10+2 / PUC in Science, Commerce, or Arts are eligible to apply.

BCA Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must have passed Karnataka 2nd PUC / 10+2 / CBSE / ICSE or equivalent examination.
  • Eligible streams: Science, Commerce, or Arts (Mathematics/Computer background is advantageous but not mandatory).
  • Minimum aggregate marks as mandated by Rani Channamma University, Belagavi.
